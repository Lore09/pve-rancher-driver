If the driver stays in `Downloading`, the cause is almost always download
related. Check in this order:

  kubectl describe nodedriver {{ $d.name }}

  - a checksum mismatch means the digest does not match the binary for {{ $tag }}
  - a timeout usually means whitelistDomains is missing a host; GitHub's
    release redirect crosses github.com, objects.githubusercontent.com and
    release-assets.githubusercontent.com, and all three are required
